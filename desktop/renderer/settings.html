Automatic recording

When turned on, Meeting Wiki starts recording as soon as you join a meeting and stops, saves, and processes it when you leave. There is no countdown before it starts. The tray icon switches to the recording icon and a notification appears while it records. A meeting that ends within 10 seconds is discarded, not processed. Both are off until you turn them on here.

Where automatic recordings go

Earlier versions saved every automatic recording to the shared wiki. That still applies until you choose an option below and save.

Private is the default. Follow my calendar uses the calendar event that matches when the recording starts, decided once and kept on retries. With no calendar, or no matching event, the meeting is private. Manual recordings use your saved defaults. Choose Start with options for a one-record override.

Zoom app

Every 3 seconds Meeting Wiki checks, on this Mac only, whether the Zoom app is running, has meeting audio connections open, or is keeping the display awake. It does not read Zoom windows, chat, participants, or meeting titles, does not use Accessibility access, and opens no network port. Zoom in a web browser is not detected.

    Google Meet in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Chromium

    Needs the Meeting Wiki for Google Meet extension. It only checks whether a meet.google.com tab shows the Leave call button and sends joined or left with the meeting code. It cannot read captions, transcripts, chat, participants, or browsing history. Turning this on installs a browser connection that only that extension can use. Events reach Meeting Wiki through a private folder on this Mac, never a network port.

      In the browser, open the extensions page, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick the folder that opens. This is needed once per browser.
